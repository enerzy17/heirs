Texas does not divide an intestate estate by who was closest at the end. With no surviving spouse it divides it equally among the children, and their children after them. The fraction does not move with the size of the estate.

Tex. Est. Code § 201.001(b), descent to children and their descendants; § 201.101, per capita with representation. Before debts, funeral, administration and the surviving spouse's community half.

If a spouse survived, the community half goes to the spouse and the children take two thirds of the separate personal property, with a remainder after the spouse's life estate in one third of the land, § 201.002(b). If the estate is under $75,000 with no administration open, ch. 205 lets the distributees skip administration entirely.
